Compare the past

Before the Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) was established, the country relied on news archives to note haze tracking.

Each circle is a year. Small and lighter was less polluted, big and dark was more with the lowest and highest reading within. Under each circle is how many hazy days that year had, then its twelve months from January (top) to December (bottom). Data from newspapers are depicted by the cross-hatched pattern. Tap a year or a month for details.

Readings before 2014 did not include PM2.5, so they cannot be compared like-for-like with later years.

Why it happened

Pick causes to show only the months where they applied. A marker shows one cause per month; hover a month for all of them. Tags are placeholders inferred from season and level, not verified attributions.

Hover or tap a cause to see what it means and why it happens.

Metric: NEA's 24-hour Pollutant Standards Index (PSI). Each day shows the highest hourly reading across the five regions (north, south, east, west, central), with the day's lowest alongside. Values are shown as published, with no smoothing or gap-filling. Records may be missing, and NEA notes the dataset has not been through its climate-data quality control. Official records: sales_climo@nea.gov.sg.

Live
NEA Real-time PSI API (data.gov.sg, v2), Singapore Open Data Licence. Requested by your browser for the current day, cached for 10 minutes and re-checked every 15 minutes while the page is open.
Saved
NEA Historical 24-hr PSI dataset (data.gov.sg, April 2014 onward), extended with the Real-time API for days after the dataset's last record. Stored as static JSON in this site and rebuilt once a day at 01:00 SGT.
Newspaper
Events before 2014 (cross-hatched) are editorial annotations drawn from The Straits Times via NLB NewspaperSG, NLB Infopedia and The Borneo Post. They are qualitative accounts, not measurements, and are updated by hand.
